Cadence Virtuoso - Transistor-Level CMOS

Design and Transistor-Level Analysis of a PLL CMOS Charge Pump

A switched-capacitor PLL control-voltage circuit with MOS-level optimization
01 / 10
Main Project Objective

Design a CMOS PLL charge pump completely at the transistor level in Cadence Virtuoso and study how device dimensions, capacitances, and UP/DN timing control Vop, which remains below VDD.

Key Parameters to Optimize
  • MOSFET Sizing ($W_n, W_p, L$)
  • Capacitors ($C_i$, $C_L$)
  • UP/DN Timing (width, gap, frequency)
  • Supply Condition ($V_{DD}$)
  • Load Current ($I_{load}$)
Target Output Figures
  • Control Voltage ($0 \leq V_{op} < V_{DD}$)
  • Total Power ($P_{avg}$)
  • Power Efficiency ($\eta$)
  • Output Ripple ($V_{ripple}$)
  • Startup Settling Time ($t_{start}$)
Team: VLSI Design Group
Department: ECE / Microelectronics
Supervisor: Project Review Committee
Implementation Level
MOS Transistor Level - Cadence Virtuoso
Reference: Sivasakthi and Radhika, “A High-Speed MCML Charge Pump Design at 10 GHz Frequency in 45 nm CMOS Technology for PLL Application.” The paper uses a 1 V supply and reports a 0-980 mV control-voltage swing, not Vop above VDD.
