Ensuring Circuit Robustness Across Process Corners, Voltage Tolerances, and Temperature Extremes
08 / 10
P — Process Corners

Fabricated MOSFET physical parameters deviate across silicon wafer runs. We test all standard PDK model corners:

  • TT (Typical-Typical): Nominal target foundry silicon.
  • FF (Fast-Fast): Lower $V_{th}$, higher mobility & speed.
  • SS (Slow-Slow): Higher $V_{th}$, higher switch $R_{ON}$.
  • FS / SF: Cross-skewed NMOS/PMOS corners.
V — Voltage Variations

Supply voltage is varied around nominal operating levels to guarantee robust operation:

  • Nominal Supply: Baseline reference $V_{IN,nom}$.
  • Low-Voltage: $V_{IN} - 10\%$ to $-20\%$ margin.
  • High-Voltage: $V_{IN} + 10\%$ to $+20\%$ margin.
  • Verification: Ensures proper startup & no breakdown.
T — Temperature Extremes

Carrier mobility $\mu(T)$ and threshold voltage $V_{th}(T)$ degrade over temperature ranges:

  • Low Temperature ($-40^\circ\text{C}$): Higher speed, higher leakage.
  • Room Temperature ($27^\circ\text{C}$): Standard nominal testbench.
  • High Temperature ($125^\circ\text{C}$): Thermal degradation & high $R_{ON}$.
Reliability Thesis
“Does the optimized transistor-level design remain fully functional and within spec when MOS characteristics, supply voltage, and temperature change simultaneously?”
