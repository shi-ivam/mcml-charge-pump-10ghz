5-Step Physical Verification Workflow
Step 1 — Physical Layout: Custom layout for NMOS/PMOS diffusion fingers, MIM capacitor arrays, guard rings, and low-resistance clock/power routing.
Step 2 — DRC (Design Rule Check): Verify geometric spacing and density rules in Pegasus/PVS. Target: DRC Clean.
Step 3 — LVS (Layout Versus Schematic): Ensure 1:1 netlist equivalence and device parameter match. Target: LVS Matched.
Step 4 — Parasitic Extraction (PEX): Extract interconnect parasitic resistance ($R_{parasitic}$) and coupled capacitance ($C_{parasitic}$).
Step 5 — Post-Layout Simulation: Re-run Spectre transient simulations using the extracted DSPF/calibre view to quantify layout degradation.
