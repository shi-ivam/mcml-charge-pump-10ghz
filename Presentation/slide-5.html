Phase 1: Store Charge
The selected switch path stores a charge packet in the internal capacitor:
$$Q_i = C_i \cdot V_i$$
Phase 2: Select Direction
UP or DN and the complementary controls select the correct charge-transfer path:
$$\mathrm{UP} \; \mathrm{or} \; \mathrm{DN}$$
Phase 3: Adjust Vop
Charge is added to or removed from CL, moving Vop while it remains below VDD:
$$0 \leq V_{op} < V_{DD}$$
Transistor-Level Non-Idealities Modeled in Cadence
1. Body Effect ($\Delta V_{th}$): Increases threshold voltage with source potential.
2. Switch Conduction ($R_{ON}$): Incomplete charge transfer within clock half-period.
3. Parasitic Capacitance ($C_{bot}$): Bottom-plate parasitic attenuates pumping factor.
4. Load Current Droop ($I_L$): Higher load current increases steady-state voltage droop.
Engineering Focus: These exact non-idealities are analyzed through parametric sweeps in Cadence Virtuoso to achieve optimum performance.
