Characterization Equations
Output Power
$$P_{OUT} = V_{OUT} \cdot I_{LOAD}$$
Input Power
$$P_{IN} = V_{IN} \cdot I_{IN,avg}$$
Power Efficiency
$$\eta = \frac{P_{OUT}}{P_{IN}} \times 100\%$$
Voltage Ripple
$$V_{Ripple} = V_{MAX} - V_{MIN}$$
Primary Optimization Target
High $V_{OUT}$ + High Efficiency ($\eta$) + Low Ripple + Low Power
