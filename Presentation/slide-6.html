Parameter What We Change Why It Matters (Physical Mechanism)
MOS Width (W) NMOS / PMOS channel widths Controls channel switch $R_{ON}$ and parasitic gate/diffusion capacitances.
MOS Length (L) Device channel lengths Affects punch-through, subthreshold leakage, and total active silicon area.
Internal Capacitor (Ci) Small, medium, and large transfer values Sets charge packet size ($Q = C \Delta V$) and the Vop step per action.
UP/DN Timing Pulse width, frequency, and timing gap Sets charge-transfer duration and prevents conflicting UP/DN paths.
Pulse Frequency UP/DN pulse-rate sweep Dictates charge transfer rate vs dynamic switching losses ($P = C V^2 f$).
Load Current (I_load) Current source load sweeps Evaluates load regulation, voltage drop, and output impedance ($R_{out}$).
Output Capacitor (CL) Filter capacitor sizing Dictates output voltage peak-to-peak ripple and transient startup settling.
Supply Voltage (VDD) Lower, normal, and higher simulated supply Validates Vop control across supply fluctuation ranges while Vop remains below VDD.
Physical Device Trade-Offs
Transistor Sizing ($W \uparrow$):
Reduces conduction loss $\to$ Higher output voltage, but increases gate parasitics & dynamic switching power.
Capacitor Sizing ($C_{pump} \uparrow$):
Transfers larger charge packets $\to$ Higher load current drive, but increases die area and startup latency.
